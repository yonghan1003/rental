Property

Used to flag current-month overdue rent. Set from 1–28.
Mortgage + maintenance fee are set once here, then auto-filled into every month's expenses. Change them any time — history keeps what you actually entered.

Lease renewal reminders

A tenant inside this window gets an amber "renew in N mo" pill and shows under Expiry watch → Start renewal talk. Lapses this month or next appear in red under Ending now. Tenants whose lease already ended are dropped from that list — they are history, and leaving them there buries what needs action.

The two windows are separate on purpose: 0 = the month they move in only (a handover is only news when it is imminent), while the renewal window lets you start talking earlier. Widening renewal will not reveal tenants three months out.

Expense categories

CategoryDefault bearerAnnual / quarterly?Tax deductible?
Bearer = You → landlord cost, reduces profit. Bearer = Tenant → recoverable or already inside the rent, so it does NOT reduce your profit. Annual items (assessment tax, quit rent, insurance) are only entered in the months they are actually paid; enter the full invoice amount in that month, or divide it yourself — the app does not guess.

Tax deductible? decides which categories reduce your taxable income on the Income Tax tab. Quit rent, IWK, repairs, assessment, insurance, agent and maintenance are pre-ticked. Mortgage is never ticked — only its interest portion is deductible, and you enter that on the Income Tax tab, so ticking it here would deduct the interest twice. Untick anything LHDN does not accept (e.g. agent commission is often partly non-deductible); it will then be listed as a real cost but not deducted.
